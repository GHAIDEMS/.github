## Who We Are

**GHAIDEMS (Ghana Innovations in Development, Education and the Mathematical Sciences)** is a multidisciplinary organisation based in Accra, Ghana, bringing together people with diverse academic, technical, and professional backgrounds to address complex developmental challenges.

Our origins in academia have shaped our appreciation for rigorous research, deep understanding, and the importance of knowledge. However, we believe that knowledge should extend beyond theory and translate into practical solutions that make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

We work with partners, researchers, educators, institutions, and communities to develop and support innovations that respond to real-world needs, particularly across Africa.

GHAIDEMS is legally registered in Ghana as a for-profit organisation, with a structure and constitution informed by a social-enterprise philosophy. This allows us to pursue sustainable business activities while maintaining a strong commitment to the communities and societies we serve.

## Our Vision & Mission

### Our Vision

**Impacting Grand Challenges.**

We aspire to contribute to addressing complex developmental challenges that resist simplistic solutions and require sustained, multidisciplinary, and context-sensitive interventions.

We recognise that these challenges are interconnected. Consequently, addressing them requires us to look beyond isolated problems and consider the wider systems, relationships, and circumstances that shape them.

### Our Mission

We work in partnership with a diverse range of collaborators to foster the growth of innovations with the potential to improve lives.

Our mission is pursued through three closely connected areas:

| Pillar | Our Focus |
|---|---|
| **Development** | Facilitating innovations and interventions that improve quality of life and contribute to sustainable development. |
| **Education** | Improving professional and academic outcomes through educational research, technology, capacity building, and innovative teaching approaches. |
| **Mathematical Sciences** | Strengthening mathematics and related disciplines as both fields of knowledge and powerful tools for understanding and addressing societal challenges. |

## What We Do

Our work spans several interconnected disciplines and application areas. We are particularly interested in initiatives that bring together development, education, and mathematical sciences.

Our areas of engagement include:

- **Software Development & Digital Innovation:** Developing and supporting digital tools, applications, and technology-enabled solutions that address practical challenges.
- **Data Science & Statistical Analysis:** Applying statistical methods, data management, visualisation, and computational techniques to generate useful insights and support decision-making.
- **Mathematical Modelling & Computational Research:** Using mathematical and computational approaches to understand complex systems and investigate real-world problems.
- **Education & Educational Technology:** Exploring innovative approaches to teaching and learning, developing digital educational resources, and supporting the integration of technology into education.
- **Agriculture & Food Security:** Supporting initiatives that promote sustainable agricultural practices, agroecology, and improved access to relevant information for agricultural decision-making.
- **Climate & Environmental Data:** Working with climate datasets, data quality assurance, climate analysis, and tools that support climate-informed decision-making.
- **Capacity Building & Knowledge Sharing:** Supporting individuals, institutions, and local partners through training, mentorship, collaboration, and knowledge exchange.

Our work is multidisciplinary by design. We believe that some of the most meaningful innovations emerge when different fields of expertise come together.

## How We Work

We recognise that complex developmental challenges rarely have universal solutions. Our approach is therefore guided by principles that encourage thoughtful problem-solving, collaboration, and sustainable impact.

### Our Guiding Principles

1. Scalable Impact: Developing solutions that can be adapted and applied in different contexts.

1. Options by Context: Recognising that solutions must respond to the realities of the environments in which they are implemented.

1. Systems Thinking: Understanding the broader systems and relationships within which problems exist.

1. Inclusivity: Valuing diversity and creating opportunities for meaningful participation.

1. Informed Decision-Making: Grounding decisions in evidence, critical thinking, and an understanding of uncertainty.

1. Collaboration and Collective Responsibility: Encouraging teamwork, knowledge sharing, and shared ownership.

Read more about our approach: [How We Work](https://www.ghaidems.org/Work.html).

- Participatory budgets and planning tools.
- Climate information analysis and communication.
- Agricultural and livelihood risk assessment.

<sub>PICSA is a participatory methodology rather than a standalone software application. Specific software tools vary by implementation.</sub>

#### STACK - Online Mathematics Assessment
- GHAIDEMS contributes to initiatives that use STACK (System for Teaching and Assessment using a Computer Algebra Kernel) to improve mathematical assessment and feedback in tertiary education.
- STACK enables educators to create interactive mathematical questions that automatically evaluate student responses and provide targeted feedback.

#### ESSA
- GHAIDEMS members contributed to a collaborative research initiative involving Education Sub-Saharan Africa (ESSA) and IDEMS International.
- The project investigated the state of statistics education in tertiary institutions in Ghana, identifying existing activities, challenges, and opportunities for strengthening statistics education.

**Research Tools & Methods**

- Semi-structured questionnaires.
- Focus group discussions.
- Institutional data collection.
- Qualitative and descriptive analysis.
- Research reporting and documentation.

<sub>This is primarily a research and data-collection initiative; a specific software implementation stack is not identified in the public project description.</sub>

Project: [ESSA](https://www.ghaidems.org/essa.html)

## Our People & Collaborators

GHAIDEMS is made up of a multidisciplinary team of researchers, educators, developers, analysts, and other professionals who bring complementary expertise and perspectives to our work.
